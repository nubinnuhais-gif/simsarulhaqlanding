Maveli Comes to Dubai

An original Onam campaign concept that brought Maveli to Dubai. The starting point was an emotional connection with Malayalis living away from home; brand awareness followed.

Inside the idea+
01

The intention

Create an emotional connection first, then build awareness of CLT Academy through a culturally familiar story.

02

The idea

Bring Maveli to Dubai. Move an iconic figure from the celebration into the world of the audience, making the story feel close to their own lives.

03

My contribution

Original campaign idea and creative direction. A story-led approach connecting a cultural occasion with a brand communication objective.

THE PERSON BEHIND THE IDEAS

Creative at heart.
Marketing in practice.

I’m Simsarul Haq, a Media & Communication Coordinator at CLT Academy in Dubai. My work brings together creative direction, strategy, and content coordination.

I enjoy finding an original idea, shaping it into a story, and working with a team to make it real. I’m drawn to creative agencies where that way of thinking can contribute to broader marketing work.

I’m also the co-founder of Rosebud Studios, an art studio. Outside work, I return to films, sketches, and photography. They give me room to follow my curiosity and tell stories on my own terms.
